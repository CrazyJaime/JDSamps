Load a tab onto a deck

  1. Open a track on YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp or Mixcloud.
  2. Click the JD Samps icon on that tab, press Alt+Shift+A, or right-click → Add this tab to JD Samps.
  3. The deck loads cued and starts buffering. Press PLAY when you're ready.
