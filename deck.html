Load a tab onto a deck

  1. Open a track on YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp or Mixcloud.
  2. Click the JD Samps icon on that tab, press Alt+Shift+A, or right-click → Add this tab to JD Samps.
  3. The deck loads cued and starts buffering. Press PLAY when you're ready.
  4. Or drop an audio file here, or paste a link to one, to load the whole track.

    Player assist briefly speeds up a tab's own player to refill a deck's reserve while it plays faster than the original. Sync itself always works on the audio.

    Load tabs as whole tracks
    Record whole track at 2× when bytes are unavailable

    A whole track takes about 22 MB of memory per minute; a longer one loads its first part. Recording at 2× is for pages whose audio cannot be read: it plays the tab through at double speed and loses the highest frequencies.
